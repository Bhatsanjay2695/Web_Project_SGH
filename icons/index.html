About Us

We are the trusted provider of industrial automation solutions

specializing in the repair, servicing, sales, and maintenance of Servo Drives, Servo Motors, AC/DC Drives, Spindle Motors, PLCs, HMIs, and Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs). With a commitment to quality, reliability, and technical excellence, we help manufacturers minimize downtime and maximize machine performance

Backed by a dedicated team of experienced engineers and a well-equipped service facility, we deliver cost-effective repair, retrofit, AMC, and engineering solutions for a wide range of industrial automation systems. We proudly support leading global brands including Bosch Rexroth, Siemens, Fanuc, Baumüller, Delta, Panasonic, and more, ensuring fast turnaround times and dependable service for customers across industries
