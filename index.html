About Us

We are the trusted provider of industrial automation solutions

specializing in the repair, servicing, sales, and maintenance of Servo Drives, Servo Motors, AC/DC Drives, Spindle Motors, PLCs, HMIs, and Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs). With a commitment to quality, reliability, and technical excellence, we help manufacturers minimize downtime and maximize machine performance

Backed by a dedicated team of experienced engineers and a well-equipped service facility, we deliver cost-effective repair, retrofit, AMC, and engineering solutions for a wide range of industrial automation systems. We proudly support leading global brands including Bosch Rexroth, Siemens, Fanuc, Baumüller, Delta, Panasonic, and more, ensuring fast turnaround times and dependable service for customers across industries

Our Services

Complete Industrial Automation Repair & Support

From diagnostics to full retrofit, we service the drives, motors, and controllers that keep your production line running.

Servo Drive Repair

Diagnosis and repair of servo drives across all major brands, with component-level fault tracing and full functional testing before dispatch.

Servo Motor Repair & Rewinding

Bearing replacement, encoder repair, and precision rewinding for servo motors, restoring performance to OEM specification.

AC/DC Drive Repair

Power stage and control card repairs for AC and DC drives, including load testing to confirm stable operation under real conditions.

Spindle Motor Repair

High-speed spindle motor overhaul including bearing upgrades, dynamic balancing, and vibration testing for CNC machine spindles.

PLC & HMI Programming

Program backup, fault diagnosis, and reprogramming for PLCs and HMIs, plus panel troubleshooting and I/O-level repairs.

VFD Repair & Retrofit

Repair and obsolescence retrofit for Variable Frequency Drives, including drop-in replacements when original models are discontinued.
